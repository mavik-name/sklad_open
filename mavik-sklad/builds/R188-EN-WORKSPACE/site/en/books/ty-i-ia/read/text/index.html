You and I...

Viktor Makarchuk · MaVik

Together... Across the distance... It sounds almost impossible, like a bridge held up not by stone, but by breath. Between us — cities. Night stations. Other people's windows. And silence, which stayed silent for so long until it learned to have a voice. I don't touch your hand, but I know when it grows cold. You don't see my face, but you can tell exactly where inside me the light breaks. We became closer than those who sleep under one roof and do not hear the heart. Distance simply stripped away the excess. Left the voice, memory, waiting and a strange honesty, when there is nothing left to hide behind. You come not through a door, not in footsteps, not with the smell of rain on a coat. You come at the moment when I stay silent longer than I can bear. And then, somewhere hundreds of kilometres away, the ice inside my chest lets go. As if someone laid a palm not on my shoulder — on life itself. And said without words: I'm here. Not beside you. Not close. Here.
