01

What is protected

Protection applies to specific original creative expression: the texts of the works, their composition, original plot lines, dialogue, fictional worlds, images, original titles, original characters and other independent original elements of the works.

If a part of a work can be used independently and is original, it may receive protection separately from the work as a whole.

02

What is not claimed as an exclusive right

The author does not claim exclusive rights over abstract ideas, concepts, facts, methods or other elements that are not themselves protected by copyright.

This notice likewise does not extend exclusive rights to biblical, historical, mythological or other persons and characters not created by the author. What is protected is the author’s particular form of depiction, interpretation, composition and other original elements.

No permission is granted for complete or substantial copying, republication, systematic reproduction, distribution, translation, reworking, adaptation, dramatization, creation of audiobooks, stage or audiovisual versions, screen adaptation, or other commercial use of protected materials.

No licence is granted to use original characters, dialogue, plot lines or other protected elements in derivative works, except where such use is expressly permitted by applicable law.

04

Automated use and AI

Making materials publicly accessible does not constitute permission for mass automated copying, systematic extraction, or the inclusion of complete or substantial portions of the texts in training, learning or other datasets for artificial-intelligence systems.

Such use requires the right holder’s prior consent unless applicable law expressly provides otherwise.

05

Quotation and permitted use

This notice does not restrict quotation, free use, or other copyright exceptions and limitations expressly provided by law. Where required by law, the author and source must be identified.

The presence of a complete text on the site, or the ability to download an EPUB, does not transfer economic rights, grant a licence to create adaptations, or authorise commercial use.

06

Legal basis

Ukraine. Law of Ukraine “On Copyright and Related Rights” No. 2811-IX of 1 December 2022. The law expressly provides protection for an independently usable original part of a work, including an original title and original character.

Berne Convention. Berne Convention for the Protection of Literary and Artistic Works, Paris Act of 1971. Ukraine acceded on 25 July 1995; the Convention entered into force for Ukraine on 25 October 1995. Article 5(2) establishes protection without mandatory formalities.

WIPO Copyright Treaty (WCT). The 1996 WIPO Copyright Treaty supplements the international copyright system in the digital environment. Ukraine acceded on 29 November 2001; the Treaty entered into force on 6 March 2002.

TRIPS. The Agreement on Trade-Related Aspects of Intellectual Property Rights. Article 9 refers to the principal provisions of the Berne Convention and confirms that copyright protection extends to expression, not to ideas as such. Ukraine has been a WTO member since 16 May 2008.
