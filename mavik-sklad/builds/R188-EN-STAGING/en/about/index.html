The work

Makarchuk writes across psychological, philosophical, mystical, war, political, documentary and children’s fiction, as well as thrillers, novellas, a parable novel, apocryphal prose and poetry.

Across those different forms, the recurring centre is the person: freedom, fear, love, faith, responsibility, technology and the right to make one’s own choice.

The principle behind mavik.name

“I don’t think of my creative work as a product. I give it to the people who need it. If something I wrote stays with someone, that is already enough.”

That is why the full texts on mavik.name are open to read without charge. Support is voluntary and comes after the work, as a reader’s response rather than a condition of access.
