Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 03

Marta read it again.

Five points.

One page.

Maybe even Viktoriia would not complain.

She was about to save the file when she remembered Lucas.

Not a specific phrase.

Not his face.

His hands.

How at the workshop he had barely touched the children’s work without permission.

How he never stayed alone with the children.

How he did not take photos.

How he did not hand out gifts directly.

How he did everything right.

To the letter.

That was exactly what bothered her.

People who behave correctly by accident usually make a mistake somewhere.

Lucas did not make mistakes.

She added a sixth point:

6. Rules do not replace attentiveness. If an adult’s behavior feels troubling, even without a formal violation, tell the responsible person.

She looked at that one longer than all the others.

Then saved the file.

The name came out simple:

Volunteer_Guide_Boundaries.docx

Marta closed the laptop.

And realized she already knew how her day would begin.

At the station, Serhii sat at his desk looking as if the battery had personally offended his professional dignity.

A folder, several printouts, and a paper cup of coffee that had long since stopped being hot lay in front of him.

“We’ve got a wonderful hero,” he said when Marta came in. “Nazar’s stepfather. Denies stealing the battery, but gives explanations as if even he doesn’t believe himself.”

“The mother?”

“Crying. Says she knew nothing.”

“Did she?”

Serhii looked up.

“Are you asking as a police officer right now, or as the person who saw her kitchen yesterday?”
