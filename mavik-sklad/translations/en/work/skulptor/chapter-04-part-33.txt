Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 33

“Is that a ‘leave him’?”

“It’s an ‘I have no right to remove him.’”

“And personally?”

Marta looked at the door.

Beyond it was the corridor.

In the corridor—Lucas.

Maybe.

Maybe not.

“Personally, I don’t know who he is,” she said. “And that’s the first honest answer I’ve had in two days.”

Olena nodded.

“That’s a place you can start from.”

When Marta returned to the workshop, Lucas was not in the corridor.

For a moment, she felt relieved.

Then worse.

Because now his absence meant something too.

She went into the room.

The children were shaping clay. The volunteers were trying not to get in the way. Petro had made something that looked like a cup, but a very gloomy one.

“What is that?” Marta asked.

“The budget,” he said. “Empty, but with a handle.”

Kateryna laughed.

Nazar sat at the edge of the table.

A small clay object lay in front of him. Not a flashlight. Not a square.
