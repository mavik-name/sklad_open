Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 23

Lucas suddenly said from the far table:

“And people who don’t take offense at rules.”

Everyone turned toward him.

He did not raise his voice.

He simply added the sentence where it was needed.

Olena nodded.

“Yes. That’s a separate point. If boundaries offend you, you’re better off not working with children.”

Petro wrote something on his sheet.

“That should be in bold.”

Marta looked at him.

“I thought you came to monitor the budget.”

“I’m multifunctional.”

Kateryna laughed quietly.

The tension eased a little.

Marta felt that the orientation was working.

Not perfectly.

But it was working.

And again she could not help noticing: Lucas was not pulling the attention toward himself. He could have said more. People would have listened.
