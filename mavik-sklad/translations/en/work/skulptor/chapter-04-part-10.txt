Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 10

Serhii was silent.

That was unusual.

“What?” Marta asked.

“Nothing.”

“Say it.”

He rubbed a hand over his face.

“I just thought that sometimes we spend so long explaining duties to children that they start blaming themselves even for the fact that we showed up to do our jobs.”

Marta looked at him.

“That’s almost philosophy.”

“Don’t get used to it. Slipped out by accident.”

He went back to the papers.

But his face had grown heavier.

The documents arrived forty-seven minutes later.

Marta opened the email in her work inbox.

Lucas Andriiovych Martyn.

Date of birth.

Passport.

Registered address.

Phone number.

Certificate.

Volunteer application.

Letter of recommendation from the Warm Hands charity program.

The name of the program struck her as unfortunate.
