Only a brief, unpleasant feeling of recognition—not in her mind, but in her body. As if somewhere in a dark room someone had quietly turned a key in a lock.

“Better.”

Olena answered on the third ring.

“I’m listening,” Olena said.

“This is Marta Koval. I need a copy of Lucas Martyn’s application and the documents for his volunteer clearance. As part of preparing the guide and reviewing the program’s safety.”

“Officially?”

“Yes.”

“All right. I’ll send them to your work email. But I’ll tell you right away: everything was normal with him on our end.”
