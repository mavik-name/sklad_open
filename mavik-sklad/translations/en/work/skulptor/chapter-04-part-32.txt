Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 32

“When I told him his mother had called, he asked, ‘Is she alone or with him?’”

Marta closed her eyes.

“God.”

“Yes.”

For a few seconds, neither of them spoke.

Through the wall came the muffled movement of the children’s workshop: chairs, voices, brief laughter, water in little jars.

Life did not stop even when adults had to decide whether a child could see his mother.

“Should Lucas be removed from the program?” Olena asked suddenly.

Marta opened her eyes.

“Why are you asking?”

“Because I see your face after you talk to him.”

“That’s not a criterion.”

“But it is a signal.”

Marta put Nazar’s list on the desk.

“Formally, there are no grounds. His documents are clean. He follows the rules. The children don’t tense up around him. The opposite.”
