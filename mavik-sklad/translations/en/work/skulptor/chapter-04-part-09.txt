THE SCULPTOR
Viktor Makarchuk · MaVik

Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 09

Marta closed her eyes.

“The batteries?”

“The batteries. The loaf of bread. The chocolate bar. The tea at the station. And he also wrote: ‘police time.’”

“Police time?”

“Yes.”

Marta did not know what to say.

Olena spoke more quietly.

“I told him police time isn’t something you return. He asked why. I said because it’s their job. He answered, ‘Then I owe the state.’”

Serhii, sitting at the next desk, looked up.

Marta realized he had heard.

“What did you tell him?”

“That the state will somehow survive. Then I gave him breakfast.”

“Did he eat?”

“Half.”

“Is that good?”

“For today—yes.”

Marta opened her eyes.

“Thank you.”

“I’ll send the documents within the hour.”

“All right.”

She ended the call.

Serhii was silent.
