Chapter 5. The Witness
Part 13

Not everything could be put on him.

That was what made it harder.

“Give me an example,” Marta said.

“There was a girl. Sofia. Fourteen. Her father was dead, her mother drank, and her mother’s partner beat both of them. The girl ended up at the center after another call. After the workshops with Luka, she became calmer. Very calm. We were pleased. Thought the therapy was working.”

“And in fact?”

“In fact, she decided her fear was an unnecessary piece that had to be cut away.”

Serhii frowned.

“What does that mean?”

“She went to a supervised meeting with her mother. The partner came too, though he wasn’t supposed to. He started shouting. Before, Sofia would hide. That time she just stood there and looked at him. Then she said, ‘You can’t break me because I already removed everything soft myself.’”

Marta felt an unpleasant chill.

“And?”

“He hit her.”

“Hard?”

“Yes.”

Yakiv closed his eyes for a moment.

“She didn’t scream. Didn’t cry. Didn’t run. Just stood there. Then she said to her mother, ‘See? It doesn’t hurt anymore.’”

Someone in the reading room turned a page.

The sound was small.

But Marta heard it far too clearly.

“That isn’t evidence against Luka,” Serhii said.

“I know.”

“The child could have come to that wording herself.”

“She could have.”
