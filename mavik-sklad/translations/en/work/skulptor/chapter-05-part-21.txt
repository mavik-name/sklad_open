Part 21

“We’ll make a copy, photograph both sides, and record that you voluntarily provided it for review. The original stays with you.”

Yakiv did not relax completely.

But a little.

“All right.”

“And you’ll write a statement,” Serhii added.

“Officially?”

“You called us.”

Yakiv nodded.

“I’ll write it.”

“Without ‘he doesn’t age,’” Serhii said. “Facts only: where you worked, when, under what name, what happened, who you can identify.”

“And if the facts don’t explain the main thing?”

“Welcome to our job,” Serhii said.

Marta carefully photographed the picture.

Front.

Back.

Then the front again, closer.

Luka Myron’s face still remained not quite clear enough.
