Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 26

This time she almost laughed.

Not with amusement.

At the absurdity of it.

“You’re either the most brazen person I’ve ever met, or the most honest.”

“Those things are not mutually exclusive.”

“Do you do this deliberately?”

“Yes.”

She stopped.

Because for the first time, he admitted it.

Simply.

Without evasion.

“What exactly do you do deliberately?” she asked.

“I don’t let you put me into a category too quickly.”

“Why?”

“So you’ll look.”

“I’m already looking.”

“No. You’re checking. That’s different.”

Marta did not answer.

Behind the glass, the volunteers were taking seats at the tables. The children were choosing places. Nazar stood beside Olena with the flashlight in the pocket of his old sweater. He was not holding it in his hands.

Not anymore.

It was little.

And a lot.

“You said the right thing today,” Lucas said.
