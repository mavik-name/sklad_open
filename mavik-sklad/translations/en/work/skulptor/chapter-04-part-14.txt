Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 14

That was better.

Second point:

Gifts only through the responsible person. A child should not feel a personal debt to a volunteer.

Third:

Do not photograph faces. Do not publish names. Do not tell someone else’s story in the child’s place.

Fourth:

Do not stay alone with a child. Safety matters more than convenience.

Fifth:

If a child speaks about danger, tell the responsible adult. Do not promise to keep that kind of secret.

Sixth:

Trust not only the rules, but your attention. If something in an adult’s behavior troubles you—say so.

At the bottom she added:

Help must not require gratitude from a child.

That one she kept.

Even if Viktoriia said it sounded harsh.
