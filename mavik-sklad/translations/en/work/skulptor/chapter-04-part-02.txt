Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 02

Serhii replied:

Don’t start.

She almost smiled.

Then returned to the guide.

The first point wrote itself:

1. A child has the right not to speak.

Marta stopped.

It was right.

And unbearably simple.

She added:

A volunteer does not pull stories out of a child, ask intrusive questions, or force a child to explain fear, silence, or refusal.

The second point:

2. Gifts are given only through the responsible staff.

The third:

3. No photos of faces, names, or personal stories without an official need and permission.

The fourth:

4. A volunteer does not remain alone with a child.

The fifth:

5. If a child speaks about danger, it is not a secret between the child and the volunteer. It is a signal for the responsible adults.
