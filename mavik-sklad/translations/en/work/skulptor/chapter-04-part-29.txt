Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 29

Someone laughed behind the glass. One of the boys was showing Kateryna how to make a dragon. Petro sat over a lump of clay with the expression of a man whose budget had just begun taking revenge on him by hand.

Marta stepped closer.

“How do you know that phrase?”

“I don’t know which phrase you mean.”

“Don’t lie.”

Lucas looked straight into her eyes.

“I haven’t started yet.”

Cold moved down her spine.

Not sharply.

Slowly.

As if someone had opened a door into a room where no one had been for a long time.

“Was that supposed to reassure me?”

“No.”

“Then what was it?”

“A mistake.”

He said it without drama.

For the first time, Marta heard him use a word that was not trying to sound precise.

Just a word.

Mistake.

“You make mistakes?”
