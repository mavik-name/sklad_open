Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 06

He watched her for another few seconds, then pulled the keyboard closer.

“Last name, first name.”

“Lucas Martyn.”

“Patronymic?”

“I don’t know.”

“Date of birth?”

“It’s in Olena’s application. I’ll request a copy officially for the program’s volunteer screening.”

Serhii nodded.

“Now that sounds like work. Not hunting.”

“I’m not hunting.”

“Good.”

He turned back to the papers.

“But if it turns out your Lucas is simply a strange man with normal documents, you’re not going to turn him into the devil.”

Marta froze.

Serhii did not notice immediately. Then he looked up.

“What?”

“Nothing.”

“I meant it figuratively.”

“I understand.”

She lowered her eyes to the monitor.

The devil.

The word landed somewhere it was not supposed to.

It did not frighten her.
