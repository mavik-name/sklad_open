Chapter 5. The Witness
Part 16

Serhii was looking at the photograph.

“You said his documents were in order. In the name Luka Myron?”

“Yes.”

“Do you have copies?”

Yakiv shook his head.

“No. Back then I didn’t think I would need them.”

“And now?”

“Now I’ve spent twenty years thinking I should have kept something. Anything.”

Marta exhaled slowly.

“Why were you silent for so many years?”

Yakiv looked at her.

“Because everything I could say sounded exactly like what you’re thinking right now.”
