Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 36

A man with documents, a job, recommendations—and no unnecessary digital noise.

In the modern world, that was almost indecent.

Marta closed the search.

Then opened it again.

Typed:

Lucas Martyn form workshop

There were more results this time.

One old announcement.

Three years old.

“Form Workshop: Clay, Silence, Boundaries. Facilitator — L. Martyn.”

Location: a youth center attached to a monastery in another city.

Marta opened the page.

The photo was blurry. A group of people around a table. Children or teenagers. In the background—the facilitator, half turned away from the camera.

His face was blurred.

But the posture was the same.

Marta zoomed in.

Pointless.

The pixels broke apart.

Under the photo was a short description:

“Form is not what we impose on material. It is what the material allows us to see.”

Marta slowly moved the phone away.

The sentence could have belonged to any craftsman.

Any art therapist.

Any man who loved clay and beautiful phrasing a little too much.

But it belonged to Lucas.

She knew that.

The phone vibrated in her hand.

Unknown number.

Marta watched the screen until the call was almost gone.

Then answered.

“Lieutenant Koval.”

For a few seconds, there was silence.

Then a man’s voice said:

“Don’t look for him in the past.”

Marta stood up.

“Who is this?”

“You won’t find anything there.”

The voice was not Lucas’s.

Older.

Hoarse.
