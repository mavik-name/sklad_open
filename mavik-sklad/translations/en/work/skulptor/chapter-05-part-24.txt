Chapter 5. The Witness
Part 24

“You’re describing help.”

“I’m describing stone.”

The word landed on the table heavier than the photograph.

Marta felt something answer inside her.

Not a thought.

A premonition.

“A child is not stone,” she said.

Yakiv looked at her as if that were the exact sentence he had been waiting for.

“Tell him that.”
