Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 30

“Yes.”

“Often?”

Lucas looked through the glass at the children.

“Less often than I should. More often than I can forgive myself for.”

Marta did not know what to say.

At that moment, the workshop door opened and Olena came into the corridor.

“Marta, can I have you for a moment?”

Marta kept looking at Lucas for another second.

Then stepped back.

“We’re not finished.”

“I know,” he said.

This time she did not correct him.

Olena led her into the small office beside the workshop.

Nazar’s sheet with the list of debts lay on the desk.

“He wants you to give this to Serhii,” Olena said.

Marta picked up the sheet.

Beside police time, Nazar had put a question mark.

Below it he had added:

If it’s expensive, I can pay it back in parts.

Marta looked at the words for a long time.

Then sat down.
