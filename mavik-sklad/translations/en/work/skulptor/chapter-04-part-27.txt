Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 27

“Which one? I’ve been unnaturally useful today.”

“About the safe hour.”

Marta looked through the glass.

“That was Olena’s thought.”

“But you accepted it.”

“Because it’s precise.”

“And incomplete.”

“What does that mean?”

Lucas was silent for a few seconds.

“A safe hour can save a day. But if afterward the child is returned to an unsafe life, adults start confusing respite with victory.”

Marta felt the familiar anger rise quickly, almost eagerly.

“This again?”

“Yes.”

“You’ve latched onto this subject.”

“No. It latched onto you.”

“You have no right—”

“I know.”

He said it before she finished.

And that threw her off more than an objection would have.
