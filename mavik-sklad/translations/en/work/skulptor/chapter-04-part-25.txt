Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 25

“No.”

She finally turned around.

Lucas stood a few steps away. Not too close. Correct again.

“I checked your documents,” Marta said.

“I know.”

“Olena told you?”

“No.”

“Then how?”

“You’re looking at me with less suspicion today, but more irritation. That means you didn’t find any formal reason.”

Marta inhaled slowly.

“You understand that sentences like that don’t help me trust you?”

“I understand.”

“And you say them anyway.”

“Yes.”

“Why?”

Lucas looked at the child’s drawing.

“Because you aren’t looking for reassurance. You’re looking for precision.”

Marta tightened her grip on the folder of guides.

“Don’t turn me into a text you can read.”

“I’m not reading you.”

“No?”

“No. You underline a lot yourself.”
