Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 05

“Olena says yes. Passport, volunteer application, recommendation.”

“Then what’s wrong?”

Marta did not answer at once.

Because the answer—he talks as if he knows where a person hurts—would have sounded bad in a police station.

Even to Serhii.

“He’s too correct,” she said.

Serhii blinked slowly.

“Is that a new risk category?”

“Sometimes, for people around traumatized children.”

“Marta.”

“I’m not going anywhere I have no right to go. But I want to check the public records, compare the application, the recommendation, the address. No freelancing.”

“And if everything’s clean?”

“Then everything’s clean.”

“Can you leave a person alone if everything’s clean?”

She looked at him.

Serhii was not smiling.

The question was not a joke.

“I’m learning,” she said.
