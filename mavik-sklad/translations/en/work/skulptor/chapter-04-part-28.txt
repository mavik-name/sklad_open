Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 28

“Then why?”

“Because you’ll keep doing your job well as long as that question hurts.”

“And if I burn out?”

Lucas looked at her.

For the first time in the conversation, there was neither challenge nor precision in his eyes.

Only something very old.

“Then you’ll start calling the ashes wisdom.”

Marta froze.

The sentence was too close to Serhii’s.

You’ll arrive at the real fire with cold ashes in your pocket.

She had never told Lucas that sentence.

Serhii had said it at the station.

Not in front of him.

“What did you say?”

Lucas seemed to realize he had crossed a boundary.

Not an external one.

Another kind.

“That burnout often disguises itself as experience.”

“No. You said something about ashes.”

“It’s a common image.”

“Not for me.”

He was silent.
