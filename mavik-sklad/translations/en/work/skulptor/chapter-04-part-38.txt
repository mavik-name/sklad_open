Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 38

Marta put the phone on the table.

The room was quiet.

The soup was cooling on the stove.

Nazar’s list of debts was in her bag.

On the laptop screen, the old announcement was still open:

“Form Workshop: Clay, Silence, Boundaries.”

Marta looked at the words and thought that boundaries did not exist only to keep danger out.

Sometimes they were needed to understand the moment danger was already inside.
