Chapter 4. The Guide

Because she was almost certain he was looking at her.

And she did not want to see that he had noticed that.

The new volunteers arrived at four.

Five people from the church: Kateryna, two students, a young married couple, and Petro, who announced that he had come “not to volunteer, but to monitor the budget,” but sat in the circle anyway.

Olena gave a short orientation.

Marta handed out the guides.

Lucas sat apart at a separate table, preparing materials: paper, clay, aprons, small jars of water. He did not interfere.

It was right.

And very irritating.

“First rule,” Olena said, “you are not coming here to rescue children.”

Petro raised his eyebrows.

“Then what are we doing?”

Olena looked at Marta.

“Helping give them one safe hour,” Marta said. “That’s enough to start.”
