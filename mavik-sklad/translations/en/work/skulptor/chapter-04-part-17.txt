Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 17

For Nazar, the difference was something else: one set had been taken without permission, the other received—but still from adults.

“Because one set you took yourself when you didn’t know what else to do,” she said. “And the other was given to you by people who wanted to help. Not in exchange. Not for anything. Just because a child shouldn’t have to sit in the dark.”

He looked at her seriously.

“What if I still want to pay it back?”

“Then someday you’ll help someone else. Not me. Not the fund. Someone who’s in the dark.”

Nazar thought about it.

“Does that count?”

“I think so.”

“Who does the counting?”

Marta did not get the chance to answer.

A voice came from the doorway:

“Not everything important needs to be counted.”

Lucas stood in the corridor.

No coat. Dark sweater. A box of paper in his hands.
