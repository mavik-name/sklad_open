Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 22

“Second. Don’t give children your phone numbers, social media, or personal promises. If you really want to help a particular child, talk to Olena. Not to the child directly.”

The young man from the married couple asked:

“What if the child asks for the number?”

“Especially then—no.”

“Why?”

Petro muttered:

“Because we just read that.”

Marta ignored him.

“Because a child in crisis can become attached very quickly to an adult who gives them a little warmth. If you are not prepared to be a stable part of that child’s life under the center’s rules, don’t create the illusion of personal rescue.”

Kateryna lowered her eyes to the guide.

“That sounds harsh.”

“Yes.”

“But right.”

“That too.”

Olena nodded.

“That’s exactly why we need rules.”
