Chapter 5. The Witness
Part 08

A real old photograph on matte paper.

The edges were slightly worn.

The picture showed a group of people in a large room. Tables. Clay. Teenagers. Two adults beside a flip chart.

One of them was Yakiv, about twenty years younger.

The other stood sideways.

Dark sweater. Sleeves rolled up. Long fingers stained with clay. His face was not fully in the frame.

But enough of it was.

Marta did not take the photograph at once.

First she looked at Serhii.

He had already set aside the upside-down book and was coming toward them.

“Who is that?” Marta asked.

“Back then he called himself Luka Myron.”

Serhii came over and stood beside her.

“May I?”

Yakiv nodded.

Serhii took the photograph by the edge and studied it for a long time.

“Year?”

“Two thousand four.”
