Chapter 5. The Witness
Part 15

Marta did not take her eyes off him.

“Did you talk to him?”

“Yes.”

“And what did he say?”

Yakiv gave a bitter smile.

“That fear does not always need to be healed. Sometimes it needs to be taught to serve.”

Marta clenched her fingers.

It sounded like Lucas.

Too much like him.

“Is Sofia alive?” she asked.

“Yes.”

“Where is she?”

“I don’t know. After that incident, the program was shut down. Luka disappeared. His documents were in order. There were no complaints. Formally, the man was guilty, child services were guilty, the mother was guilty, I was guilty, everyone was guilty. Just not Luka. He hadn’t broken a single rule.”
