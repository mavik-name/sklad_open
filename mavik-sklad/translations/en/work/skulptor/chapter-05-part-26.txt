Chapter 5. The Witness
Part 26

It was almost offensive.

When something inside begins to crack, you want something outside to happen too. The sky to darken. A lamp to flicker. People to stop.

But no.

The world always had more composure than a person.

The phone in her pocket vibrated.

Marta took it out.

A message from Olena:

Nazar refused to meet with his mother today. He said: “I’m not ready to be good yet.”
