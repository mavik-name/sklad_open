Serhii looked at her more closely.

“Did you sleep?”

“A little.”

“That’s ‘no’ in the form of self-respect.”

“I was looking for old mentions of Lucas.”

“And?”

“There’s an announcement from three years ago. ‘Form Workshop.’ He’s the facilitator.”

“Three years ago isn’t an exposé. It’s a résumé.”

“The photo is blurry.”

“Crime of the century.”

“Serhii.”

“Marta, I’m on your side. But I’m not going to help you break procedure because you’re scared.”

She looked up.

“I’m not scared.”

“Worse. You’re curious.”

That hit harder than it should have.

“This isn’t curiosity.”

“Then what is it?”

Marta looked at the sticky note with the number.

“He’s always on the boundary. Formally, everything is right. The documents exist. He follows the rules.”
