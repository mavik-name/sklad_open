“Nothing you could file a complaint about,” he said at last. “That was the problem.”

Marta sat opposite him.

“Be specific.”

“Luka didn’t hit the children. Didn’t touch them inappropriately. Didn’t stay alone with them. Didn’t ask for money. Didn’t take photographs. He followed the rules better than any of us.”

Serhii gave Marta a brief look.

She did not react.

“Then where was the danger?” she asked.

Yakiv placed his hands on the table.

“He knew how to make a child start believing that their pain had a form. And if it had a form, then something could be carved out of it.”

Marta felt the word form rise between them again.

“That doesn’t necessarily sound bad,” Serhii said.

“I thought so too.”

“And then?”
