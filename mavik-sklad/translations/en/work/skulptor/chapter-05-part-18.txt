Chapter 5. The Witness
Part 18

“Did you see him after 2004?” Serhii asked.

“Once.”

“When?”

“In 2012. Another city. Another program. Another name.”

“What name?”

“Lev.”

Serhii gave Marta a brief look.

She sat motionless.

“Last name?”

“I don’t remember. Or I don’t want to remember it wrong. It was from a distance. I saw him on a stage. A workshop for teenagers. He was talking about how a crack is the place where material shows the truth about itself.”

“And you didn’t go up to him?”

“No.”

“Why?”

Yakiv lowered his eyes.

“Because I was afraid he would recognize me.”

“And did he?”

“He did.”

Marta felt the air between them grow denser.

“How do you know?”

“He didn’t stop the talk. Didn’t change his voice.”
