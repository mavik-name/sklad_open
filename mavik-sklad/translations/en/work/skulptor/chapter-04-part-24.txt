Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 24

He knew how. Instead, he stayed silent until his sentence became necessary.

That was either the sign of a good volunteer.

Or of very dangerous discipline.

After the orientation, the volunteers dispersed to look at the workshop.

Marta stayed in the corridor beside a display of children’s drawings. On one sheet, a hand was holding a light bulb. The bulb was shining not yellow, but blue.

Under the drawing, in a child’s handwriting, were the words:

“The light is cold, but it’s there.”

Marta looked at the words for a long time.

“This isn’t Nazar’s,” Lucas said.

She did not turn around.

“I didn’t ask.”

“But you thought it.”

“You’re doing it again.”

“No. This time I just guessed.”

“Don’t you ever get tired of being unbearable?”

“You’ve asked that already.”

“And?”
