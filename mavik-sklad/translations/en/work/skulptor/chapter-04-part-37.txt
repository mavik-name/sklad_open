Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 37

As if the man were speaking either after an illness or after a long silence.

“Identify yourself.”

“He isn’t Martyn.”

Everything inside Marta became cold and precise.

“Who are you?”

“Someone who once thought he was just a strange volunteer too.”

“How did you get my number?”

A pause.

“If you want to understand who he is, don’t read the documents. Watch what happens to people who start believing him.”

“Identify yourself.”

The connection cut off.

Marta kept the phone to her ear for a few more seconds.

Then slowly lowered her hand.

The number was still on the screen.

Unknown.

But not hidden.

She took a screenshot.

Then called Serhii immediately.

He answered in a sleepy voice after the fifth ring.

“If this isn’t a corpse, a fire, or the end of the world, I’m going to hate you tomorrow.”

“I just got a call about Lucas.”

A pause.

The sleep disappeared from his voice.

“What did they say?”

“That he isn’t Martyn. And that I won’t find anything in the documents.”

“Did the number come through?” Serhii asked.

“Yes.”

“Take a screenshot and send it to me. Not to ‘run it,’” he added at once. “For the record. Tomorrow we’ll call from the work phone. If there’s real information about a risk to children, we’ll proceed officially.”

“Serhii.”

“What?”

Marta looked at the dark window.

“Is this grounds now?”

Serhii was silent for two seconds.

“Now,” he said, “at least it isn’t only your feeling anymore.”

The call ended.
