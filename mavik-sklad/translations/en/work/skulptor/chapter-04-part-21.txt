Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 21

The student with short hair raised her hand.

“What if a child starts telling us something difficult on their own?”

“Listen,” Marta said. “Don’t interrogate. Don’t let horror show on your face. Don’t say, ‘you poor thing.’ And don’t promise, ‘I won’t tell anyone.’ If it’s about danger, tell Olena or the responsible staff member.”

“What if the child gets upset?”

“They might.”

“And then what?”

“You handle it.”

The girl fell silent.

Marta softened her tone.

“Trust doesn’t mean keeping every secret. Trust is when, over time, a child understands that you did not use their words against them.”

Lucas looked up.

She noticed.

But continued.

“Second.”
