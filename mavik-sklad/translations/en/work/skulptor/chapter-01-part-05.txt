THE SCULPTOR
Viktor Makarchuk · MaVik

Chapter 1. Dirt on a Child’s Hands
Part 05

She opened the car, put the bag on the passenger seat, but did not get in.

“Why did you say that? About the child and the other room.”

Lucas did not answer at once.

He looked at her as if deciding how much truth a person could be given before she would stop thinking this was a chance conversation.

“Because today you almost let yourself think that a good act does not always defeat evil. And it wasn’t the evil that frightened you. You were frightened that good might not be enough.”

Marta felt her throat go dry.

“You don’t know anything about me.”

“Of course.”

“Don’t talk as if you do.”

“All right.”

And again he did not argue.

That irritated her most of all.

Marta got into the car and closed the door. The cabin met her with cold. She started the engine.

Lucas was still standing under the snow.

For a moment it seemed to her that behind him the coffee shop, the pharmacy, the road, the passersby—everything had become slightly less distinct. As if the city were a painting and someone had dragged a finger through paint that was still wet.

Marta blinked.

Everything became ordinary again.

She swore at herself silently for being tired.

Before pulling away, Marta looked once more at the bag with the flashlight.

The batteries clicked softly against each other when the car shuddered.

A small dry sound.

Like someone dropping two pebbles into dark water.

That night Marta did not yet know that some people do not enter your life through a door.

They ask a question.
