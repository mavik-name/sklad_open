Chapter 3. Fund Meeting
Part 09

Marta read the message.

Once.

Then again.

Then she sat on the edge of a chair.

Everything that had happened over the last two days suddenly came together not into a picture, but into a knot.

Nazar.

The flashlight.

The center.

Clay.

Lucas.

Her father.

Her mother.

The meeting.

Children who could not be photographed because their pain was not proof of someone else’s mercy.

She set the phone on the table.

Outside, it was getting dark.

The hall was still lit, but now the light seemed less certain than it had a few minutes ago.

Marta stood, switched off the lamp beside the flip chart, and headed for the exit.

The box of markers remained on the table.

Black.

Blue.

Red.

Cap to cap.

Neat.

Too neat.

And for some reason that troubled her most of all.
