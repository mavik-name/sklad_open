Chapter 4. The Guide
Part 18

Marta slowly turned her head.

“You’re here today too?”

“Today is the new-volunteer day. They need materials.”

Olena, standing by the door, said calmly:

“I called him. Don’t look at him as if he crawled out of the ventilation system.”

Nazar looked at Lucas.

“If you don’t count, how do you know you paid it back?”

Lucas did not enter the room. He stayed by the threshold.

Good.

He remembered the boundaries.

“Sometimes you know not when the debt is gone,” he said, “but when you stop carrying it in place of your heart.”

Nazar did not understand.

Marta did.

And became angry again.

“Too complicated for a child,” she said.

Lucas nodded.

“Yes.”
