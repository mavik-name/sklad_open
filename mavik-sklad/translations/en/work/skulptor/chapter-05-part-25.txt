Chapter 5. The Witness
Part 25

Yakiv wrote the statement slowly.

Serhii sat beside him and did not hurry him. Every so often he only clarified a date, the name of the center, Sofia’s name, positions, surnames Yakiv remembered or did not remember.

Marta stood by the window.

She could see the city from above: wet pavement, people with umbrellas, a bus stopped at a traffic light, a woman with a red bag crossing the road where she should not.

The world below looked ordinary.
