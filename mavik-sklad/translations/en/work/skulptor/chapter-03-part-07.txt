THE SCULPTOR
Viktor Makarchuk · MaVik

Chapter 3. Fund Meeting
Part 07

“Tomorrow.”

Her mother opened the door.

“And Marta.”

“What?”

“You don’t have to be the only person in the room who sees danger.”

Marta wanted to answer.

She could not find anything to say.

Viktoriia left.

Only Lucas remained in the hall.

Of course.

Marta was almost no longer surprised.

He stood by the table, putting the markers into a box. Black, blue, red. Cap to cap.

“Do you do this on purpose?” she asked.

“Clean up?”

“Stay where I have to walk past you.”

“No. Someone just has to put the markers away.”

“There’s always someone in a church who’ll put the markers away.”

“That’s why they get lost so often.”

She stopped by the table.

“My mother says I’m sharp with you.”

“Your mother is precise.”

“Don’t start.”

“All right.”

He closed the box.

Marta looked at his hands. They were clean after the clay.

Nothing mystical about them.
