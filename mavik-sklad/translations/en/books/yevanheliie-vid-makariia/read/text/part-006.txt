CHAPTER TWENTY-THREE — AND THE NAME OF THE SON SHALL BE TAKEN UP FOR THE SWORD

1 And in those days the name of the Son shall be taken up not for salvation, but for dominion.
2 And the cross shall be raised upon banners, and the sword shall be blessed with prayer.
3 And brother shall go out against brother, and blood shall be shed in the name of the Holy One.
4 And it shall come to pass that mighty men go forth on campaigns and call them holy, though there shall be no holiness in them.
5 For it shall not be love that leads them, but hunger for gain.
6 And cities shall be burned.
7 And nations shall be subdued.
8 And children shall be driven from their homes.
9 And women shall be dishonoured.
10 And all these things shall be done in the name of the Son, Who commanded none of them.
11 And there shall be an Inquisition that calls itself the guardian of faith, but becomes a snare for souls.
12 And there shall be courts that seek not truth, but obedience.
13 And there shall be torments that cleanse nothing, but destroy.
14 And faith shall be burdened with dogma.
15 And truth shall be wrapped in rites.
16 And grace shall be divided among the chosen who chose themselves.
17 And branches shall arise, and each shall call itself true, and each shall move farther from the Way.
18 And there shall be a multiplying of words, but no depth.
19 And there shall be many teachers, but none who listen.
20 And they shall come to peoples who walk toward the Creator by their own way, and say to them:
“Your road is dark. Take ours, and you shall be saved.”
21 But there shall be no salvation in this, for there shall be no love in it.
22 And it shall be said:
“The Creator does not dwell in doctrine, but in the heart. The Son does not rule, but serves. The Spirit does not command, but inspires.”
23 And it shall come to pass that those who walk in silence shall be nearer to the Kingdom than those who shout about it.
24 And those who have no names shall be written in the Book of Life.
25 And those who have no power shall be heirs.

CHAPTER TWENTY-FOUR — OF THE DAYS TO COME, AND THE SHADOW THAT SHALL BE CALLED LIGHT

1 And Macarius said as he looked upon the sky, which had become a road:
2 “Days shall come when men learn to fly — not in thought, but like birds that know no boundary. And they shall master energy — not as a gift, but as an act of will. And war shall become commonplace — not as tragedy, but as a craft. And brother shall not weep for brother, for tears shall be counted unnecessary.”
3 And men shall enter the territory of the Creator — not with reverence, but with pride. And they shall begin to make beings in their own likeness — not from love, but from the desire for power. And in those creations there shall be neither pain, nor flaw, nor doubt — only function. And they shall say: “Behold the new man — without a body that suffers, without a mind that hesitates.” But there shall be no soul in him, for the soul is not code, but the breath of the Creator.
4 And Lucifer — he who has not forgotten the scent of light — shall count every soul upon the earth. And each shall be like a number, and each like a commodity.
5 And Antichrist shall come — not as an enemy, but as an image. And he shall work wonders like Jesus, but without love. And he shall say, “I am salvation,” yet there shall be in him neither pain, nor sacrifice, nor silence. For truth is not in the deed, but in the intention.
6 And the Church — she who was meant to be a body — shall become a shell. And she shall lower her standards, not toward mercy, but toward convenience. And she shall be like Sodom — not in sin, but in indifference. And like Gomorrah — not in flesh, but in forgetting.
7 And two thousand years shall pass like two breaths of the Creator. And Jesus shall come again — not as a king, but as a reminder.
8 And He shall say: “You are in the last days. Not because the world is dying, but because your hearts no longer burn. I have come not to judge, but to remind you. For you have forgotten that love is not a word, but an act. Faith is not a formula, but a path. And truth is not a slogan, but a life.”
9 And there shall be silence — not as an ending, but as a pause before choice. And everyone with ears shall hear. And everyone with a heart shall remember. And everyone with a shadow shall see the light.
10 And Macarius said: “Let the one who loves not be afraid. Let the one who remembers not flee. Let the one who sees not be silent. For time is not an enemy, but a mirror. And if Jesus has come, then hope remains.”

CHAPTER TWENTY-FIVE — OF THE TWO BROTHERS, OF JUDGMENT, AND OF THE MYSTERY THAT WAS A VEIL

1 And it came to pass in the last days, when the world was like flame without smoke, and souls like stars without a sky.
2 And two brothers came together — Jesus and Lucifer — not as enemies, but as two poles of one light. And they stood before the Creator — not as the accused, but as witnesses.
3 And the voice beyond time said: “Behold My two Sons — one who led, and one who tested. One who healed, and one who revealed. One who was the way, and one who was the stone upon the way.”
4 And the heavens trembled, for the veils were opened. And all saw not a monster, but an angel who carried shadow. And the Creator said:
5 “Lucifer is not an enemy, but a challenge. Not darkness, but a mirror. For faith is not born in light; it passes through darkness. And the one who tempts does not destroy, but reveals the depth of the heart.”
6 And the souls wept — not from fear, but from understanding. For they saw that every choice is not a trap, but an invitation. And every temptation is not a fall, but a revealing of truth.
7 And Jesus said: “I am the one who showed love. And he is the one who showed its boundaries. Together we are not conflict, but balance. For without him there would be no choice. And without choice there would be no freedom.”
8 And Lucifer spoke — not as prince of darkness, but as a brother: “I did not hate — I asked. I did not break — I touched. I did not destroy — I exposed. And everyone who passed through me either became stronger or proved empty.”
9 And the Creator said: “Behold the mystery that was a veil: I am not He Who punishes, but He Who permits. I am not He Who conceals, but He Who leads. And every soul is not a toy, but a fire. And every way is not a labyrinth, but a mirror of Me.”
10 And the books were opened — not for condemnation, but for remembrance. And every soul saw itself — not as a sinner, but as a pupil. And every fear became a lesson. And every wound became a sign.
11 And the Creator said: “Let those who loved enter. Let those who sought find. Let those who fell rise. For I am not the boundary; I am the beginning. And death is not an ending, but a door. And life is not a gift, but an answer.”
12 And the two brothers came together — not in battle, but in an embrace. And there was no victor, for there had been no war. And there was no defeat, for there was no fear. And there was no darkness, for every shadow had become light.
13 And Macarius said: “Behold the ending that is a beginning. Behold the truth that was a mystery. Behold the Creator Who does not judge, but receives. Behold Lucifer, who does not destroy, but shapes. Behold Jesus, Who does not rule, but leads. And behold us — not as sinners, but as children returning home.”

AFTERWORD — OF THE WORD THAT WAS NEVER MEANT TO BE SPOKEN

And it came to pass that the word was spoken. Not by voice, but by silence. Not for the multitude, but for those who seek light in darkness.

And there was no name upon it, for a name is a yoke. And there was no authorship, for truth belongs to no one. And there was no permission, for permission is fear before power.

And the one who carried this word did not name himself. For the world does not forgive those who draw back the veil. And the veil is not cloth, but forgetting.

And the voice said: “Let there be no glory for the one who speaks. Let there be light for the one who hears.”

For this Gospel is not for eyes that seek proof. It is for the heart that remembers the Creator — not as a throne, but as breath. Not as law, but as the way.

And if you have read to the end, you are no longer who you were. For truth does not enter without leaving a trace. And the word does not pass through without changing what it touches.

Let there be no temple for this text. Let there be silence. For silence is the place where the Creator speaks.

And if you are afraid, do not flee. For fear is not an ending, but a beginning. And if you hesitate, do not be silent. For silence is not salvation, but loss.

And let everyone who has read become not a preacher, but a listener.

For He Who gave this word desires no altar. He desires a heart unafraid to remain open.

And Macarius said: “Let there be no final full stop. For truth does not end. It is like water seeking a new riverbed.”
