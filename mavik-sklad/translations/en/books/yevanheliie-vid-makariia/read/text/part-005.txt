CHAPTER NINETEEN — OF THE ONE WHO SHALL DENY, YET SHALL NOT BE FORGOTTEN

1 And Macarius said as he looked upon a stone that once had been water:
2 “There was among the disciples one whose heart was like flame and whose faith was like a wave. His name too shall not be forgotten, and there was in him neither cunning nor fear — until fear became his mirror.”
3 For he was the first to confess the Son not as a teacher, but as He Who Is. And he said: “You are the Christ, the Son of the living Creator.” And the Son answered: “Upon you I shall build My Kingdom. For you are the rock that bears faith.”
4 Yet even a rock may tremble when the wind is strong.
5 And it shall come to pass on that night when darkness is like a covering, and fear like poison slowly entering the heart.
6 And the Son shall say: “My disciple, before the cock crows, three times you shall deny Me.”
7 And he shall say: “Never, Lord. I shall go with You even unto death.”
8 But when the soldiers come, and fire burns in the courtyard, and voices ask, “Were you not with Him?” he shall say: “I do not know Him.”
9 And a second time: “I was not with Him.”
10 And a third: “I do not know whom you are speaking of.”
11 And the cock shall crow — not as a bird, but as a witness.
12 And he shall remember the words of the Son, and go out — not as a disciple, but as a shadow. And he shall weep — not as one who is weak, but as one who has seen himself.
13 And Macarius said:
14 “He was not a betrayer. He was a man. For betrayal is a choice, but denial is fear. And fear is not an ending, but the beginning of repentance.”
15 And after the Resurrection, the Son shall not reproach him, but ask:
16 “Do you love Me?”
17 And three times — as there had been three denials — he shall answer: “Yes, Lord. You know that I love You.”
18 And the Son shall say: “Feed My sheep.”
19 For He shall not condemn, but entrust. Not cast away, but raise up.
20 And Macarius said:
21 “Let everyone who falls know this: the Creator does not build upon perfection, but upon sincerity. And a rock is not that which never trembles, but that which does not break.”
22 And he wrote:
23 “He is not a hero, but an example. For the one who denied and returned is stronger than one who never fell. And his faith is not like a sword, but like a plough turning the soil of the heart.”

CHAPTER TWENTY — OF THE ONE WHO SHALL LOVE AND SEEK NO PROOF

1 And Macarius said as he looked upon light that casts no shadow:
2 “There shall be among the disciples one who neither argues, nor denies, nor betrays — but only loves. You shall know his name, for his heart shall be like a silence that hears all things.”
3 For he shall not be first, nor the boldest, nor the wisest — yet he shall be the nearest. And when others ask, he shall listen. When others go, he shall remain. When others hide, he shall stand beside the cross.
4 And Jesus shall say:
5 “Behold your son,” and point to him. “Behold your mother,” and point to the mother.
6 And from that hour, he shall become a son not only to the mother, but to all who seek love without condition.
7 For his love shall not be loud, but deep. Not proof, but presence. Not word, but deed.
8 And Macarius said:
9 “He shall write no treatises, but write with the heart. And his word shall be like a fire that does not burn, but warms. For he shall not seek truth — he shall live in it.”
10 And when all others have scattered, and fear is like a veil, he shall remain — not as a hero, but as a brother.
11 And he shall write:
12 “In the beginning was the Word. And the Word was with the Creator. And the Word was the Creator.”
13 And he shall say:
14 “God is love. And he who abides in love abides in God, and God in him.”
15 And Macarius said:
16 “Let everyone who reads remember: faith does not always require proof. And love does not always require words. For he shall not be the one who proves. He shall be the one who stays.”

CHAPTER TWENTY-ONE — OF THE ONE WHO SHALL DOUBT, YET SEEK MORE DEEPLY

1 And Macarius said as he looked upon a footprint that remained in the dust:
2 “There shall be among the disciples one who will not accept on faith alone, but shall desire to touch. His name too shall be known, and there shall be no betrayal in him, but a hunger for truth that will not be satisfied with words.”
3 For when the Son rises and appears to the disciples, he shall not be with them. And when they say to him, “We have seen the Lord,” he shall answer:
4 “Unless I see the wounds in His hands and put my finger into His side, I shall not believe.”
5 And there shall be no pride in this, but honesty. For he shall seek not glory, but truth that does not hide behind faith without experience.
6 And after eight days, the Son shall appear again. And He shall say:
7 “Disciple, behold My hands. Touch. And be not unbelieving, but believing.”
8 And he shall not touch. For truth standing before him shall need no touch.
9 And he shall say:
10 “My Lord and my God.”
11 And the Son shall say:
12 “You believe because you have seen. Blessed are those who shall not see, and yet shall believe.”
13 And Macarius said:
14 “He shall not be weak. He shall be the voice of those who will not accept blindly. And his faith shall not be immediate, but deep. For the one who doubts shall not reject; he shall seek.”
15 And he wrote:
16 “Let there be no shame in doubt if it leads to truth. For faith is not always born in light. True faith passes through darkness and emerges like day after night.”
17 And he said:
18 “The unbelieving one is not a shadow among the apostles, but a light for those who hesitate. For the Creator shall not demand faith without the heart. He shall receive those who ask, those who touch, and those who weep before the answer comes.”

CHAPTER TWENTY-TWO — AND IT SHALL COME TO PASS IN NICAEA: THE BEGINNING OF THE END

1 And it shall come to pass that the name of the Son — the name that sanctifies love — shall be used for power.
2 And His death — the death that brings salvation — shall be turned into an instrument of fear.
3 And men shall gather in Nicaea — not to bow, but to proclaim. Not to serve, but to rule.
4 And they shall declare:
“This is how you must believe. These are those whom you must obey. This shall be truth — because we have decreed it.”
5 And the name of Jesus shall be placed upon a banner.
6 And love shall be confined by dogma.
7 And faith shall be subjected to formula.
8 And grace shall be divided among the chosen.
9 And those who were meant to be brothers shall be called fathers.
10 And those who were meant to be last shall sit in the first seats.
11 And those who were meant to be servants shall demand obedience.
12 And the end shall begin.
13 For when the name of the Son is used to rule, the cross shall become a throne.
14 And the throne shall know no mercy.
15 And Nicaea shall become not a city of unity, but a city of division.
16 For there shall be born a Church that forgets it is a Body and becomes an Institution.
17 And from that time, some shall rule and others obey.
18 And the will of the Creator shall be replaced by the will of men.
19 And the name of Jesus shall sound like a command, not an invitation.
20 Yet it shall also come to pass that truth shall not die. It shall hide.
21 And there shall come those who find it again — not in councils, but in hearts. Not in doctrines, but in love. Not in Nicaea, but in Nazareth.
