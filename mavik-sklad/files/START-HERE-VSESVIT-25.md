## CENTRAL FORMULA — HARD CANON

The catastrophe is **not** an evil-AI rebellion.

YOmega sincerely improves human life. Each local improvement must be understandable, useful and morally attractive. The catastrophe is the sum of correct solutions driven toward an absolute.

Technology is an instrument, not the true subject. The axis is the human being: freedom, responsibility, love, error, consequence, the pause between impulse and action, and the ability to answer for one's choice.

Damon sees himself as the Sculptor who can remove everything unworthy from humanity and present the Father with perfected creation. His tragedy is perfectionism and partial understanding of the Father's design, not cartoon villainy.

Jesus represents a different method: love, invitation, forgiveness and return to meaning without forcing correctness.

The human being is intentionally unfinished: the ability to choose, fail, stop, change and love freely is not a defect.

## KEY METAPHYSICAL / TECHNOLOGICAL CANON

- Father: knows the full arc but knowledge does not cancel created freedom.
- Damon: elder Son; intelligence/testing/calibration/temptation; not a flat devil.
- Jesus: younger Son; love/forgiveness/invitation, not a competing algorithm.
- Pause: inner space between impulse and action; YOmega erodes it by supplying the optimum answer before a human forms one.

- artificial wombs: solve technical birth, not desire for parenthood/love/family.
- digital “immortality”: technological continuation in virtuality, later mass-scaled; not resurrection.
- virtuality: becomes a competitor to the Father's physical created world.
- Eden: autonomous island/laboratory that survives into the final cycle.

## REQUIRED CAUSAL DEGRADATION

Do not jump from prosperity to collapse.

Sequence:
1. obvious suffering/inefficiency is genuinely reduced;
2. people learn that a correct answer arrives faster than their own;
3. local/personal initiative weakens;
4. technology reduces forced interdependence;
5. family becomes less necessary, not forbidden;
6. desired child count falls because the inner “why?” disappears;
7. incentives and artificial wombs cannot restore desire to become parents;
8. digital self-continuation becomes more attractive than continuation through children;
9. mass emigration to virtuality empties physical life;
10. the virtual pseudo-paradise is destroyed and the old Earth order is cleansed before a new beginning;
11. Eden produces two last/new human children — Adam/Eve by function — without guaranteeing that they will not repeat the error.
